Data-Driven Niche Selection: Build Your Digital Brain In 30 Days

Every time you hit publish, you are adding a new data point to your library of content—your “Digital Brain.”

This is how you scale yourself on the Internet, and:

  • Discover your niche: The more you write online, the more your niche will reveal itself to you.
  • Share your story: In the age of AI and infinite text-based commodity content, one of your greatest differentiators is sharing who you are (which AI can’t do) and how you see the world.
  • Build your Personal Language Model: The more stories and insights you share online, the more you will “train your brain” on your category, personality, and writing style.

Not sure what your niche is, or what you want to write about?

Don’t worry! As part of Ship 30 for 30, we will give you a 30-day roadmap to follow—so you know exactly what to write, and how to write it, every single day.

Multimedia Creation: Become A Full-Stack Writer

Next, we aren’t just going to “write.” We’re going to build a library of multimedia assets!

Our goal is to help you master the 4 most popular writing formats in the digital age:

  • Short-Form: Bite-sized thoughts compressed into 280 characters or less.
  • Atomic Essays: 300-word visual essays to master the fundamentals of writing online.
  • LinkedIn Carousels: Slideshows that grab readers' attention and have the highest likelihood of going viral on LinkedIn.
  • X Articles: 800+ word blog posts that can be republished across X, your blog, Medium, even your weekly newsletter.

If you can write in these 4 formats, you can write anything.

(And we’ll show you how you can republish your writing across The Big 3 text-based social platforms: X, LinkedIn, and Substack.)

AI Agent Distribution: Write Once, Publish Everywhere

You do the writing, the robots do everything else.

The larger your Digital Brain gets, the more you can scale your ideas online!

  • Build your content library: Think of everything you write yourself as “approved language” for AI to use and leverage on your behalf. But it’s your responsibility to do the writing!
  • Remix your content across platforms & formats: The same piece of writing can be compressed, expanded, copy/pasted, and remixed into different formats and types of media.
  • Hire a team of AI Agents to do the low-leverage work for you: The larger your Digital Brain gets, and the more “approved language” you have to play with, the more you can just focus on writing net-new material (while the robots do everything else).
